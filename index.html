About Our Institution

Nurturing Leaders for Sindh & Pakistan

Global Public School Naudero was established with a singular noble vision: to deliver world-standard, values-based education to the youth of Naudero and surrounding districts of northern Sindh.

From our modest beginning in 2008, GPS has grown into a benchmark school known for exemplary academic standards, disciplined character formation, modern computer science laboratories, and vibrant sports clubs. We cultivate critical thinkers who are grounded in their cultural heritage while equipped to excel in a globalized world.

Academic Rigor

Curriculum aligned with international benchmarks and Board syllabus.

Modern STEM & Coding

Digital computer lab and robotics hands-on training from Grade 3.

Character & Islamic Values

Moral integrity, respect, empathy, and social civic responsibility.

Sports & Co-Curricular

Competitive athletics, cricket, badminton, debating, and art.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding admissions, school timings, examinations, transport routes, and school policies.

Our regular school timings are Monday to Thursday and Saturday from 7:45 AM to 1:45 PM. On Fridays, the school dismisses at 12:00 PM for Jummah prayers. Montessori and kindergarten wings finish 30 minutes earlier to ensure relaxed pickup for younger children.

Global Public School Naudero is formally registered with the Sindh Education & Literacy Department and officially affiliated with the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) Larkana for SSC Part I & II (Matric Science and Computer Science groups).

Our transport service covers Naudero town center, Station Road, Sugar Mills Colony, Al-Murtaza Colony, Garhi Khuda Bakhsh road, Banguldero junction, and designated pickup spots along the main Larkana Highway route.

Parents must provide: (1) Copy of Nadra Birth Certificate or B-Form, (2) Father’s/Guardian’s CNIC photocopy, (3) Four passport-sized student photographs, and (4) School Leaving Certificate (SLC) / previous progress report if transferring from another school.

Yes. A 15% tuition concession is provided for the second sibling and 20% for the third. Furthermore, merit-based 100% and 50% tuition waivers are granted to district top-rankers, along with a dedicated Zakat/Welfare scholarship fund for orphaned and underprivileged children.
